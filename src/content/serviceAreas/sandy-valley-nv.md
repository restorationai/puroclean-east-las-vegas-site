## Restoration emergencies common in Sandy Valley

Sandy Valley's desert setting creates a specific pattern of damage. Summer monsoon cells can drop a hard, fast rain on hardpan soil that doesn't absorb it, sending runoff toward homes built at grade rather than above it. On a property with a shallow foundation or an older roof, that water finds its way in through roof flashing, window returns, or a slab edge faster than most people expect. Between storms, the extreme heat and low humidity dry out wood framing, siding, and roofing materials, which makes them more prone to cracking and gaps that let water in the next time it rains. Many homes in the valley also rely on private wells and septic systems; a failed septic line or a pump issue can turn into a contamination problem that needs biohazard-level cleanup, not just a mop and a fan.
