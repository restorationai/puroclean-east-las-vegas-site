## Restoration emergencies common in Summerlin South

Summerlin South sits in a desert valley, so the restoration calls here follow a different rhythm than wetter climates. Most of the year, low humidity actually works in a homeowner's favor once a leak is caught early. The risk spikes during monsoon season, typically July through September, when sudden downpours overwhelm roof drainage and grade around slab foundations faster than the soil can absorb it. Flat and low-slope tile roofing common to the area can hide a slow leak for a long time before staining shows up on a ceiling below. Irrigation line breaks and slab leaks are also frequent culprits, since many homes here were built with copper supply lines that are now decades old and prone to pinhole leaks under concrete.
