## Restoration emergencies common in Winchester

Winchester's restoration calls track closely with the desert climate and the age of the neighborhood. Summer monsoon storms can dump heavy rain in a short window, and older roofs and aging flashing don't always shed that volume fast enough, which shows up as ceiling staining or water tracking down interior walls. Evaporative coolers and older HVAC lines, common on homes built before central air was standard, are another frequent source of slow leaks that go unnoticed until flooring or drywall is already damaged. Low humidity most of the year keeps surface moisture from being obvious, which is part of why water intrusion here often reads worse once materials are opened up than it does from a quick visual check.
