Commercial restoration in Summerlin South comes with its own rhythm. Office parks and retail centers along the master-planned community's commercial corridors are mostly newer stucco-and-tile construction, built to desert code, which behaves differently under water intrusion or fire damage than the older block buildings found elsewhere in the Las Vegas valley. A burst supply line behind a break room wall or a kitchen fire in a strip-mall restaurant doesn't just threaten one tenant space, it can spread through shared plenums and HOA-governed common areas before anyone notices.

## Why Summerlin South Properties See Commercial Issues

Most commercial buildings in this part of the valley went up from the 1990s through the 2000s, during Summerlin's main build-out years, which means a lot of flat and low-slope roofing systems that are now reaching the age where membrane seams and flashing start to fail. Combine that with the valley's hard summer monsoon bursts, intense UV exposure that dries out roofing sealants faster than in milder climates, and caliche-heavy soil that drains poorly after a downpour, and you get a predictable pattern: roof leaks in July and August, slab moisture intrusion after heavy rain events, and HVAC condensation issues in buildings that run cooling hard for most of the year. Stucco exteriors also mean that water finds its way behind the lath and building paper in ways that aren't always visible from inside a leased space until drywall starts to bubble.

## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Summerlin South

We start with a walkthrough that documents the affected space for the property manager, the tenant, and the insurance carrier separately, since commercial leases in Summerlin South's office and retail centers often split responsibility between landlord and tenant policies. From there, extraction and structural drying follow the IICRC S500 standard for water losses, with moisture mapping used to track how far intrusion has traveled behind stucco, under slab-set flooring, or into shared wall cavities between tenant spaces. For fire and smoke losses, we isolate the affected unit with containment barriers before cleaning begins, since retail centers and office suites usually share rooftop HVAC units that can recirculate smoke odor into neighboring businesses if the system isn't shut down early. Reconstruction, when needed, is scoped and scheduled to get the space back to a leasable or operational condition with minimal disruption to the businesses around it.

## Summerlin South Insurance & HOA Coordination

A large share of Summerlin South's commercial square footage sits inside HOA-governed business parks and shopping centers, which means restoration work often has to satisfy two sets of requirements at once: the insurance carrier's documentation standards and the HOA or property management company's rules on contractor access, work hours, and exterior appearance during repairs. We photograph and log the loss in detail from the first visit, which speeds up both the insurance claim and any HOA sign-off needed before scaffolding, drying equipment, or exterior tarping goes up on a shared building. For multi-tenant properties, we also coordinate directly with the property manager so neighboring businesses know what to expect and for how long.

## Local note

One thing that trips up out-of-town contractors working in Summerlin South: the area's stucco-over-foam exterior systems trap moisture differently than traditional stucco-over-lath. Surface drywall can test dry within a day, but the foam backing holds moisture far longer, so we extend monitoring on these buildings rather than closing out the job on a surface reading alone. It's a small distinction, but it's the difference between a clean close-out and a mold callback three weeks later.
