## Why Summerlin South Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most homes in this part of the valley sit on slab foundations, which means a supply line leak or water heater failure often shows up first as warped flooring or a damp baseboard rather than an obvious puddle, by the time it's visible, water has usually been traveling under the slab or inside a wall cavity for days. Las Vegas's notoriously hard water also plays a role: mineral scale builds up inside water heater tanks and supply fittings faster here than in most parts of the country, and that buildup is a common cause of the pinhole leaks and tank failures we get called out for. Add in the summer monsoon season, when sudden downpours overwhelm desert landscaping and grading that's built for dry conditions most of the year, and you get a second, very different kind of water event: wind-driven rain finding its way past stucco and window flashing on custom and production homes alike.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Summerlin South

The first step is always finding out where the water actually is, not just where it's visible. On slab homes that means moisture mapping along baseboards and flooring transitions before any demolition starts, so we're not cutting into finished walls that don't need it. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water off tile, hardwood, and carpet pad, followed by placement of air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage and the materials involved. Stucco exteriors and tile roofing common throughout Summerlin South behave differently than wood siding or asphalt shingle when water intrudes, so we check wall cavities and insulation before we call drying complete. Drying is monitored daily with moisture meters, following the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, rather than guessed at by feel or by how a surface looks.
