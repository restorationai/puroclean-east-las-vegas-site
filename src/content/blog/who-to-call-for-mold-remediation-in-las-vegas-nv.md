## What Happens When You Call PuroClean of East Las Vegas?

When you call (702) 551-3040, the first conversation is a triage call, not a sales pitch. Have a few things ready: the approximate size of the visible mold area, where the moisture source was or is, and whether anyone in the home has had recent respiratory symptoms.

After the initial call, the typical sequence looks like this:

1. **Mold inspection and moisture assessment** - A technician visits the property, identifies visible growth, and uses a moisture meter to map hidden wet areas behind walls or under flooring. If the extent of the mold is unclear, [mold inspection and testing](/services/mold-inspection-testing/) with air sampling or surface swabs can confirm what species are present and at what concentration.
2. **Containment setup** - The affected area is sealed with poly sheeting and negative air pressure is established using an air scrubber with HEPA filtration. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the work.
3. **Remediation** - Mold-affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, subflooring) are removed and bagged. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated. The crew works in full white Tyvek suits, respirators, and nitrile gloves throughout.
4. **Drying and structural drying verification** - If the moisture source caused any residual dampness, drying equipment runs until moisture readings return to normal. Mold cannot be permanently removed from a surface that is still wet.
5. **Clearance testing** - A post-remediation verification (PRV) confirms spore counts have returned to normal background levels. This step protects you and documents the job for your insurance file.

The timeline depends on the scope. A contained bathroom or closet infestation may be resolved in one to two days. Larger losses involving multiple rooms or structural materials take longer. PuroClean of East Las Vegas provides a written scope before work begins so you know exactly what is planned.

## Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Mold Remediation in Las Vegas?

Sometimes, and the answer depends on what caused the mold. Most standard homeowners policies in Nevada cover mold remediation when it results directly from a covered peril: a burst pipe, an appliance failure, or storm-related water intrusion. They typically exclude mold that developed from a slow, undetected leak or from chronic humidity, because those fall under the "neglect" or "gradual damage" exclusion.

Las Vegas's monsoon season, which runs roughly July through September, is a common source of covered claims. Flash flooding through a window or door, or roof intrusion from a sudden storm, can trigger mold growth within 24 to 48 hours in the desert heat. If that is your situation, document everything with photos before any cleanup begins and call PuroClean of East Las Vegas so the crew can provide the moisture readings and written scope your adjuster will need.
