## Restoration emergencies common in Goodsprings

Goodsprings sits in the southern Nevada desert on the edge of Clark County, and its restoration problems track the climate and the age of the town. Summer monsoon storms can send flash flood water down washes and across unpaved roads fast, and older structures without modern grading or gutters take on water at foundations and door thresholds. Winter brings occasional hard freezes that split exposed supply lines in buildings that were never insulated for sustained cold. Add in blowing dust and wind-driven debris that find their way into roof penetrations, and the town sees a steady mix of water damage, wind and storm damage, and the mold that follows if a leak sits unnoticed in a seasonal or vacant property.

Much of Goodsprings' building stock dates to the town's mining-era origins, with a mix of older wood-frame and masonry structures alongside newer manufactured and site-built homes added over the decades.
