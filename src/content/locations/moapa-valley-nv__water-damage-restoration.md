## Why Moapa Valley Properties See Water Damage Issues

The valley's summer monsoon pattern brings short, intense storms that the desert ground can't absorb fast enough. Add in the area's mix of older site-built homes and manufactured housing, many with pier-and-beam or raised foundations built for the desert climate, and you get water intrusion patterns that look different from what we see on a typical Las Vegas valley floor job: moisture tracking along foundation vents, insulation batts soaking through from below, and subfloor damage that isn't visible until you pull back flooring.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Moapa Valley

We start every call with a moisture assessment, checking walls, subfloor, and any crawlspace access to map how far water has traveled before we move a single piece of equipment.
