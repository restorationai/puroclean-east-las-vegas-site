## Why Winchester Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Winchester's housing stock is a mix of older tract homes from the valley's mid-century growth years and newer infill construction, and both present different fire risks. Older homes often still run on original electrical panels that were never sized for today's window AC units, space heaters, and kitchen appliances, which is a common ignition point we see across unincorporated Clark County. Evaporative cooler setups, popular throughout the area for decades, add another wrinkle: swamp cooler wiring and roof penetrations are frequent culprits in attic and roofline fires, and the ash from those fires tends to settle into insulation that's already dried out from years of desert heat, making it more likely to smolder and re-ignite hours after the visible flames are out.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Winchester

We start with a damage assessment that separates heat damage from smoke and soot damage, because in Clark County's climate those two types of harm behave differently.
