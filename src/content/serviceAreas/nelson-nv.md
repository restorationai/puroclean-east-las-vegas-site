## Restoration emergencies common in Nelson

Nelson sits in a desert canyon environment, and the restoration problems we see tied to that geography are fairly consistent. Flash flooding from summer monsoon storms is the biggest one: runoff moves fast through canyon washes and can push water into structures that otherwise never see standing water, especially those built at grade or near a wash line. The dry climate the rest of the year works against homeowners in a different way, drying out seals, gaskets, and roofing materials until the next storm finds the gap. Fire risk is also real given the surrounding desert vegetation and the distance from a full-time fire crew, and older mining-era and mid-century structures in the area can carry outdated wiring that raises that risk further.
