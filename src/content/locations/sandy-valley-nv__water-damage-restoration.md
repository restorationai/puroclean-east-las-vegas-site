## Why Sandy Valley Properties See Water Damage

Sandy Valley sits in a remote stretch of the Mojave Desert in Clark County, well outside municipal water and sewer service for most properties. That means homes typically run on private wells with pressure tanks and standalone septic systems, and both are common sources of water intrusion we get called out for: a cracked pressure tank flooding a utility closet, a septic line backing up into a bathroom, or a supply line fitting that corrodes faster than expected in the area's mineral-heavy well water. Add in monsoon season, when short, intense summer storms send runoff across dry washes and low-lying lots faster than the ground can absorb it, and you get flash flooding that pools against slab foundations built for a climate that's dry almost everywhere except the two or three weeks it isn't. Because so many homes here are single-story, slab-on-grade construction, water tends to travel sideways under flooring rather than down through a basement, which changes how we approach extraction and drying.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sandy Valley

We start with water extraction using truck-mount or portable units depending on how far into the property we need to reach, since gravel driveways and longer setbacks are common on Sandy Valley's larger rural lots. From there, structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard as a benchmark, but the targets shift for desert homes: low ambient humidity can make flooring and drywall feel dry to the touch within a day while moisture is still trapped under vinyl, laminate, or against the underside of a slab. We use moisture meters and, where needed, infrared imaging to confirm what's actually happening below the surface rather than relying on how a room feels. Dehumidification equipment gets sized to the space, not just the square footage, because the extreme summer heat common to Sandy Valley means HVAC systems are often working overtime already and can't be counted on to help with drying the way they might in a milder climate.
